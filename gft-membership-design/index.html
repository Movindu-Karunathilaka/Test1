Garage Full Throttle · customer app · design proposal 3

Membership refresh

The same treatment as Home and Appointments, applied to the Membership tab. The points system, tiers, price and perks stay exactly as they are. What changes is how they're shown: a Club card, a progress track to the next tier, a calculator that sells the membership with your own numbers, and a proper member area once someone joins.

Kept: Tier 1 under 1,000 · Tier 2 1,000+ · Tier 3 2,750+ Kept: 10% / 15% / 20% off Kept: 1 pt per $100 vs 5 pts per $10 Kept: $19/month (was $29)

  1. 1

    The GFT Club card Medium

    Replaces: "GFT Club · Your point balance 20" text box

    A card that changes with the customer's status: an empty dashed outline before they join (still showing their 20 points), red for Tier 1, silver for Tier 2, gold on black for Tier 3. Members can show it at the counter or add it to their phone's wallet.

  2. 2

    Tier track, drawn to scale Small

    Replaces: "How it works" numbered text

    Your actual thresholds (0, 1,000 and 2,750 points) on one line, with a marker for the customer's points and the gap to the next tier in points and in dollars of servicing. It explains the system without a "how it works" paragraph.

  3. 3

    "What would you get?" calculator Small

    Replaces: "You now 1 point every $100 / Members 5 points every $10"

    The same two earn rates, turned into a dollar answer. On a $400 job a non-member earns 4 points; a member saves $40 and earns 200. Drag the slider in the mockup. It's the strongest pitch you have, and it uses only your numbers.

  4. 4

    All tiers open, as a ladder Small

    Replaces: three accordions with Tier 2 and 3 closed

    Customers shouldn't have to tap to find out what they're working towards. Each tier shows its points, discount and perks. Members see Done, You're here or Locked on each one. The Tier 2 and 3 extras marked "Suggested" are placeholders, because those accordions were closed in the screenshots.

  5. 5

    Day-one perks as icons Small

    Replaces: "Why join" + "Included from day one" (the same list twice)

    Today the Tier 1 perks appear in "Why join", "Included from day one" and inside Tier 1. Show them once as three icon tiles: 10% off, wiper blades, headlight polish.

  6. 6

    Join bar that stays in reach Small

    Replaces: Join button at the very bottom of the page

    The price ($29 crossed out, $19/month, 34% off), the cancel-anytime line and the Join button stay pinned above the tab bar while non-members scroll. Members never see it.

  7. 7

    "Saved as a member" counter Medium

    GFT addition

    The total discount plus free perks used since joining. It's the best reason not to cancel, and it's worked out from invoices the app already has.

  8. 8

    Points activity Medium

    GFT addition

    Every job and the points it earned at 5 per $10, so members can check the maths themselves. It ties in with the History tab on Appointments.

  9. 9

    Manage membership Medium

    GFT addition

    Plan, next payment date, card and a clear Cancel link. The copy promises "cancel anytime", so make cancelling a tap, not a phone call. A "Pause a month" option keeps some people who would otherwise cancel.

04 · What changes from today

Problems with the current tab

The same three perks, three times"Why join", "Included from day one" and the open Tier 1 accordion all list 10% off, wiper blades and headlight polish. Show them once (5).
Tier 2 and 3 are hiddenThe tiers worth working towards are closed accordions. Open them all as a ladder (4).
A point balance with no meaning"20" sits alone with no sense of what it gets you. The card (1) and the track (2) put it in context.
"How it works" is textThree numbered sentences. The tier track (2) shows the same idea in one line.
US spelling"labor" should be "labour" for an Australian garage. It's a small detail, but customers notice.
Join is at the bottomNon-members only reach the price after scrolling past everything. The pinned join bar (6) fixes this.

05 · Questions for the client

Five rules to confirm before building

The screenshots don't answer these, and the design depends on them.

Do points expire?Are tiers based on lifetime points or the last 12 months? Lifetime is simpler to show.
Do non-member points carry over?If someone with 20 points joins, do they keep them? Yes is the friendlier answer, and it's what the card design assumes.
What happens on cancel?Do they keep their tier and points if they come back later?
What are the Tier 2 and 3 extras?The mockup shows suggestions, marked "Suggested". Swap in the garage's real perks.
Are points ever spent?If points only decide the tier, the "80 pts to a free wash & wax" idea from the Home mockup should become "360 pts to Tier 2".

06 · Build order

Ship it in three drops

Phase 1 · Non-member pitch

About 1–2 weeks. No backend changes.

  • Club card, empty state (1)
  • Calculator (3)
  • Tier track (2)
  • Tier ladder, day-one perk tiles (4, 5)
  • Pinned join bar (6)

Needs: confirmed Tier 2 and 3 perks.

Phase 2 · Member area

About 2 weeks. Light backend work.

  • Tier cards for members (1)
  • Points activity (8)
  • Manage membership, including cancel (9)

Needs: a points ledger per job; subscription details from the payment provider (next date, card); pause and cancel.

Phase 3 · Extras

About 2 weeks.

  • "Saved as a member" counter (7)
  • Show-at-counter QR code
  • Add to Apple Wallet / Google Wallet pass
  • Tier-up celebration screen and push notification

Needs: discount totals per invoice; a wallet pass certificate.
