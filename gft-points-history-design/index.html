    GFT addition

    One line that says how points are earned, so "+500 for $1,000" makes sense straight away. Below it: points this year, points from jobs and points from bonuses.

  3. 3

    Filter chips Small

    GFT addition

    All, Jobs, Bonuses, Tier changes and Adjustments. Try them on the busy-member example, where the list is long enough to need them.

  4. 4

    Grouped by month, with totals Small

    Replaces: one long list of cards

    "November 2026 · +520" headings make it easy to see what each month added. Rows sit together in one card rather than each in its own box.

  5. 5

    Rows in plain words, green for earned Medium

    Replaces: red "+500", "Appointment APT-000008", "$1000"

    Red usually means money going out, so earned points turn green. Amounts get proper formatting ($1,000.00). Where the appointment has a service and a car, show them ("Logbook service · Ranger") and keep the APT number as a small reference. The busy-member example shows this. This account's row still shows the APT number, because that's all the screenshot gives.

  6. 6

    Tap a row to see the maths Medium

    GFT addition

    A sheet shows the date, amount paid, rate and the sum: $1,000 ÷ $10 × 5 = 500. It has buttons to open the appointment or the invoice. When customers can check the maths themselves, they stop calling to ask about it.

  7. 7

    Tier milestones in the list Small

    GFT addition

    When a job takes the customer over 1,000 or 2,750 points, a silver "Tier 2 unlocked · now 15% off" row appears in the list. It's worth 0 points, but it's the moment worth celebrating.

  8. 8

    Adjustments, clearly marked Small

    GFT addition (only if the garage ever adjusts points)

    If points are taken back, for example after a refund, the row shows a minus in red with a plain reason. If the garage never does this, leave the chip and row type out.

  9. 9

    A nudge for non-members Medium

    GFT addition

    For a non-member, the same $1,000 job earns 10 points at 1 per $100. Under that row: "As a member, this job would have earned 500 pts", with a Join button. It uses the customer's own history to make the case.

02 · The maths, kept exactly

How this account got to 520

Worked out from the screenshot and the rules on the Membership tab. Nothing here changes your rules.

7 Nov · APT-000008+500pts

$1,000 ÷ $10 × 5 = 500
Member rate: 5 points per $10.

1 Nov · Sign-up+20pts

Sign-up bonus, given once when the account is created.

To Tier 2480pts

1,000 − 520 = 480
At 5 per $10, that's about $960 of servicing.

Same job, non-member+10pts

$1,000 ÷ $100 × 1 = 10
50 times fewer points. This is the nudge in note 9.

To Tier 32,230pts

2,750 − 520 = 2,230
About $4,460 more servicing for this account.

Rounding$10steps

The mockup counts whole $10 blocks ($678 → 335 pts). The garage should confirm how the app rounds.

03 · What changes from today

Problems with the current screen

Earned points are redRed reads as money going out. Earned points turn green; red is kept for points taken back (5, 8).
An internal ID as the title"Appointment APT-000008" means something to the workshop, not the customer. Lead with the service and car, and keep the ID small (5).
"$1000" with no explanationThe amount needs formatting ($1,000.00), and the customer needs to see why $1,000 is worth 500 points (2, 6).
A balance with no goal"520 pts" doesn't say what it's for. The tier bar does (1).
A refresh buttonPull to refresh, as on every other screen.
Every row is a separate boxFine for two entries, heavy for twenty. One card per month scales better (4).

04 · Questions for the client

Confirm before building

Before or after the discount?Are points worked out on the full price or on what the member actually paid after 10% off? The footer line in the mockup assumes after the discount. Change it if that's wrong.
Does GST count?Are points earned on the GST-inclusive total? Most customers will assume the number on their invoice.
Can points go down?If a job is refunded or cancelled, are its points taken back? If never, drop the Adjustments chip.
Do points expire?If they do, the hero card needs a line such as "120 pts expire on 1 Mar". If not, say "Points never expire" under the balance. It's a selling point.

About 1–2 weeks. Light backend work.

  • Service name and car on job rows (5)
  • Filter chips (3)
  • Tier milestone rows (7)
  • Adjustments, if used (8)
  • Non-member nudge (9)

Needs: service and vehicle linked to each points entry; an entry type field (job, bonus, tier, adjustment); tier-change events.
