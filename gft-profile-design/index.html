02 · Account rules before launch

What the stores and customers expect

Checks to make before submitting the update. This isn't legal advice; confirm the details for the garage.

Delete account in the appApple's App Store guidelines (5.1.1) require apps that support account creation to let people start deleting their account from inside the app. Google Play has a similar requirement. Explain what's kept (for example invoices the garage must keep) and what's removed.
Emailing Apple relay addressesCustomers who hide their email get an address ending in privaterelay.appleid.com. Emails sent to it only arrive if the garage's sending domain is registered with Apple's private email relay service. Otherwise invoices and receipts disappear.
Say why you collect a birthdayBirthday and mobile are personal information. The privacy policy should say what they're used for (for example a birthday treat and "car ready" calls) and the app should link to it.
Marketing stays opt-inKeep deals and offers separate from job updates and off by default. Customers will trust job notifications more if they're never mixed with promotions.

03 · What changes from today

Problems with the current tab

The customer's name is a codeWith Sign in with Apple, the name and avatar become a random relay ID like "4J…". Asking for a first and last name fixes the Profile, Home greeting and every notification (1, 5).
Negative or dead-end text"Profile picture cannot be changed", "No vehicles for now" and "No notifications yet" each tell the customer what they can't do. Each one becomes a next step (5, 7, 9).
Mark all read looks disabledIt's dark red on black, so it reads as greyed out (9).
Hours without an answerThe hours are there, but the customer still has to work out if the garage is open now (11).
Refresh buttons on sub-screensAs on every tab, pull to refresh replaces them.
No delete account or settingsOnly Log out. App review will ask about account deletion (4).

04 · Build order

Ship it in three drops

Phase 1 · Must-haves

About 1–2 weeks.

  • First and last name, profile card (1, 5)
  • Delete account (4)
  • Live open status, Copy address, today's hours (11)
  • Mobile and birthday formatting, save bar (6)

Needs: an account deletion process; the sending domain registered for Apple relay email.

Phase 2 · Garage and inbox

About 2–3 weeks.

  • Car cards with service and rego bars (7)
  • Notification inbox with types (9)
  • Notification settings and quiet hours (10)
  • Shortcut tiles, finish-your-profile card (2, 3)

Needs: odometer and rego-expiry fields; notification categories on the server.

Phase 3 · Nice to have

About 1–2 weeks.

  • Add a car by rego lookup (8)
  • Profile photo upload
  • Map preview (12)
  • Birthday-month treat, if approved

Needs: a vehicle lookup provider; a maps SDK key.
