    Spin tickets with a reset countdown Small

    Replaces: "3 spins left this week" pill, cut off under the header

    Three ticket icons that tear off as they're used, and a live countdown to Monday's reset. It's the same 3-a-week rule, shown as something the customer can count at a glance.

  2. 2

    The wheel is a tyre Medium

    Replaces: a flat red-and-black pie

    Tread blocks around the edge, "Garage Full Throttle · Spin to win" lettered on the sidewall, an alloy hub with five wheel nuts, and an icon on every prize. The same eight prizes in the same order. Only a garage could own this wheel.

  3. 3

    A spin that feels real Medium

    Upgrade of: "Spin the wheel" button

    Five fast turns that slow down onto the prize, with a light tick of haptic feedback per segment on the phone. The server decides the prize before the animation starts, and the wheel just lands on it, so nobody can cheat by changing the app.

  4. 4

    Result sheet with a next step Small

    GFT addition

    A win slides up a sheet with the prize, its use-by date and a button to use it on a booking. "No luck" gets its own calm version showing spins left, without confetti. Try a few spins in the mockup.

  5. 5

    My prizes wallet Medium

    GFT addition (the biggest gap today)

    Today there's nowhere to see what you've won. Each prize becomes a ticket with a code and a use-by date. Customers show it at the counter or pick it in the booking flow, and the garage marks it used in the admin panel.

  6. 6

    Plain-English prize names Small

    Replaces: labels like "Tyres 4+1" and "Scan report"

    The wheel keeps its short labels, but the ticket and the prize list say exactly what you get. "Tyres 4+1" means buy 4, get the 5th free? The garage needs to confirm (see section 02).

  7. 7

    Recent spins, with what happened Small

    Replaces: "No spins yet" box

    Each spin with its day and result, and whether the prize was saved or used. It updates as you spin in the mockup.

  8. 8

    Out-of-spins state Small

    GFT addition

    When the three are gone, the wheel greys out with "Back Monday" and a countdown, instead of a button that does nothing. An optional bonus spin for booking a service is marked "Suggested", because it changes the rules.

  9. 9

    Rules, odds and app store wording Small

    GFT addition (needed before launch)

    A short line under the wheel linking to terms and odds, including the statement that Apple isn't a sponsor. Apple asks for this in its App Store rules for contests. See section 04.

03 · The wheel and the tickets

Drawn as a tyre, kept as a ticket

  • Tread60 tread blocks around the edge. In the app they can motion-blur while the wheel spins and sharpen as it slows.
  • Sidewall"Garage Full Throttle · Spin to win" lettered around the tyre, the way a brand name sits on a real one.
  • RimA thin red lip between the tyre and the prizes, the same red as the brand.
  • PrizesAlternating red and charcoal, as today. Each has an icon, a short headline and a second line.
  • HubAn alloy centre with five wheel nuts. The GFT mark stays upright while everything else turns.
  • PointerA red pin with a white outline, so it reads clearly against both segment colours.

04 · Before you ship

Rules for a prize game in an app

A free spin-to-win wheel counts as a promotion with prizes. These are the checks to make before launch. This isn't legal advice; the garage should confirm the details.

Decide prizes on the serverThe app asks the server for a result, the server records it, and only then does the wheel animate. A result picked inside the app can be faked.
App Store rules for contestsApple's review guidelines (section 5.3) require official rules in the app and a statement that Apple isn't a sponsor. Google Play has its own contest policy, so check that too. The terms line under the wheel covers Apple's wording.
Australian promotion rulesFree games of chance with prizes can count as trade promotion lotteries. Permit rules differ between states and depend on the prize pool, so check for every state customers might play from.
Publish terms and oddsWho can play, spins per week, when they reset, how prizes are chosen, prize use-by dates, and what "No luck" means. Link both from the Spin tab.
Free to playKeep spins free. Bonus spins for booking are fine to consider, but they shouldn't be the only way to play. Check this with whoever reviews the terms.
Track redemptionsEach prize gets a one-time code. Staff mark it used in the admin panel, so the same ticket can't be used twice.

05 · What changes from today

Problems with the current tab

The spins pill is cut off"3 spins left this week" slides under the header. It's replaced by the ticket row (1).
Wins have nowhere to goThere's no list of prizes won, no code and no use-by date. The wallet (5) fixes this.
A filler text box"Tap spin for a chance to win…" takes a card's worth of space to say what the button already says. It becomes one line with a rules link.
Cryptic prize names"Tyres 4+1" and "Scan report" need explaining. The tickets and prize list do that (6).
A refresh buttonAs on the other tabs, pull to refresh replaces it.
No rules or oddsThere's no terms link yet, and launching without one risks App Store review problems (9).

06 · Build order

Ship it in three drops

Phase 1 · Look and feel

About 1–2 weeks.

  • Tyre wheel, pointer, spin animation (2, 3)
  • Spin tickets with countdown (1)
  • Result sheet (4)
  • Terms and odds line (9)

Needs: written terms; odds from the garage.

Phase 2 · Prize wallet

About 2 weeks. Backend work.

  • Server-decided results
  • My prizes tickets with codes and use-by (5, 6)
  • Recent spins (7)
  • Mark-as-used in the admin panel

Needs: a prize table with odds and use-by; one-time codes; redemption logging.

Phase 3 · Joined up

About 1–2 weeks.

  • "Use a prize" step in the booking flow
  • Out-of-spins state + bonus spin, if approved (8)
  • Push notification on Monday reset
  • Countdown on the Home carousel (already in the Home proposal)

Needs: agreement on bonus-spin rules.
