  1. 1

    Points chip in the header Small

    LMCT+ has: coin balance chip (10k) top-left

    Today the point balance only shows on the Membership tab. Put it on every screen's header; tapping it opens Membership. Swap the refresh button for pull-to-refresh, and put the GFT wordmark in the middle like LMCT+ does with its logo.

  2. 2

    Welcome back, by name Small

    LMCT+ has: "Welcome back, Ethan Crowley!" with photo

    "Hey there" becomes the customer's first name, with a Club badge for members or a "Join GFT Club" pill for everyone else. Sign in with Apple can hide the real name (the Profile tab currently shows "4jjbtybd7c"), so ask for a first name once during onboarding.

  3. 3

    Reminder strip Small

    LMCT+ has: "Access days left · 673" strip

    The garage version of a countdown that matters: kilometres to the next service, days until rego renewal, or "your car is ready". It shows whichever is most urgent, and tapping it books or opens the job.

  4. 4

    Promo carousel with a live timer Medium

    LMCT+ has: Daily Reward banner with countdown and slide dots

    Keep the current neon-car image, but give it a job. Slide 1 counts down to the weekly spin reset and links to Spin. The other slides are monthly specials and the Club offer, managed by the garage from the admin panel. Swipe the banner in the mockup to try it.

  5. 5

    Round quick actions Small

    LMCT+ has: Leaderboard · Special Offers · Achievements

    Book, Call us, Deals, Rewards. Booking stays the red primary action. This row replaces the two large buttons and frees about 120 px of screen height for the new content.

  6. 6

    Points card with a rev-counter Medium

    LMCT+ has: Sept EXP / 2025 EXP / coin balance with a 3D coin

    Points this month, points this year, and the balance. Where LMCT+ shows a coin, GFT shows a tacho dial that fills toward the next free reward. "80 pts to a free wash & wax" gives people a reason to book again.

  7. 7

    Live job tracker Small

    GFT addition

    The app already has the statuses (Created, Approved, In Progress, Ready to pick up) with their colours. Show them as a progress line on the active appointment, so customers stop calling to ask "is it done yet?".

  8. 8

    My garage with service and rego meters Medium

    GFT upgrade of "My vehicles"

    A Victorian-style plate, the model, and two bars: next service and rego renewal. Needs odometer and rego-expiry fields on the vehicle. These also drive push reminders, which bring people back without needing a daily reward.

  9. 9

    Deals this week Medium

    LMCT+ has: Discounts carousel from partner businesses

    Start with GFT's own deals: brakes, tyres, add-ons. Leave one slot for local partners later (a café, a car wash, a detailer). Partners can pay for the slot or swap promotion.

  10. 10

    Badges and Refer a mate Large

    LMCT+ has: Achievements

    Simple milestones such as first booking, 3 services, spin winner and referred a mate, each worth bonus points. Referral is the one with a direct payoff for the garage: the friend gets $20 off their first job, and the customer gets 50 points once that job is completed.

02 · Background options

Something better than flat black

All six keep the cards solid, so text contrast doesn't change. The texture shows in the header area and the gaps between cards. Tap a tile to apply it to the phone above.

For the developers: each background is a 375 × 812 SVG in assets/backgrounds/. Place it at the top of the scroll view on every tab, full width, over a solid #0B0B0D, and let it scroll with the content. It fades to solid black at the bottom, so longer screens need nothing extra. Use the same treatment on Appointment, Membership, Spin and Profile so the tabs feel like one app.

03 · Build order

Ship it in three drops

Phase 1 uses data the app already has, so the client sees a new Home quickly. Later phases add backend work.

Phase 1 · Quick wins

About 1–2 weeks. Uses existing data.

  • New background on all tabs
  • Points chip in header (1)
  • Welcome by name + Club badge (2)
  • Round quick actions (5)
  • Live job tracker (7)
  • Better empty states for new users

Needs: a first-name field at onboarding.

Phase 2 · Engagement

About 2–3 weeks. Light backend work.

  • Promo carousel managed by the garage (4)
  • Points card with progress dial (6)
  • Service and rego meters + push reminders (3, 8)
  • Deals this week (9)

Needs: banner and deal admin (image, text, link, start/end date); monthly and yearly point totals; reward thresholds; odometer and rego-expiry fields on vehicles.

Phase 3 · Growth

About 3–4 weeks. New features.

  • Badges with bonus points (10)
  • Refer a mate with share link (10)
  • Local partner deals

Needs: referral codes and deep links; rules for awarding badges; a partner deal type in the admin.

04 · What not to copy

Parts of LMCT+ that don't suit a garage

LeaderboardLMCT+ is a big national community. One garage ranking its own customers by spend would feel odd and shows other people's activity. Badges give the same sense of progress without the comparison.
Daily check-in rewardsPeople visit a garage every few months. A daily reward trains them to open the app and close it again. The weekly spin already covers this, so the carousel promotes the spin instead.
Removing "Call the garage"It moves into the quick-action row but stays one tap away. Many garage customers still prefer to ring.
Empty screens for new usersIn today's app, a new customer sees "No appointment for now" and "No vehicles for now". Each empty block should become a prompt with a reward, such as "Add your car, +10 pts". Switch the mockup to "Brand-new sign-up" to see it.
